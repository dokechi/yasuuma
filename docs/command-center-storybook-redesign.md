# The working storybook — original full presentation redesign

Baseline: `d2f356068378651cbe9f98c277310e2fbed64ed4` (including lottery `@chusen1101`). The user explicitly rejected the model-like 3D finish and requested a comprehensive, colorful KOKUYO Curiosity-is-Life-inspired redesign. This is not a palette swap around the old castle, another optional prototype, or a claim to reproduce the reference's production scale.

## Reference and independent design

- https://www.kokuyo.com/special/curiosity-is-life/
- https://mount.jp/projects/kokuyo-curiosity-is-life/

The official maker's stills were inspected. Their live WebGL interaction could not be reliably completed in the available reference browser, and is not claimed as witnessed. Color samples are image-derived representative values, not official brand specifications. No logo, name, proprietary character, illustration, model, music, or source code is incorporated.

An original open working notebook is the default presentation: broad pale space, saturated chapter color pairs, crisp ink outlines, folding paper scenery and recognizable original companions redrawn into the same illustration language. Five chapters differ in structural composition, not just tint. On phone, a full-size single page replaces the wide spread; it never shrinks the entire desktop scene into miniature.

## Ownership and continuity

Existing account, notebook, desk, search, favorites, copy-history, pending-revision, native sourcing and authentication owners remain authoritative. Source IDs, account handles, local notes and persistence keys are unchanged. All main imports use one canonical owner URL to prevent duplicate ESM initialization inherited from mixed cache pins.

The illustration/page carrier may fold and move; the live manuscript and controls are never bent or perspective-distorted. Canonical work routes commit immediately. Five explicit direct routes and reduced-motion settings have zero travel delay. Presentation is inert, pointer-transparent, cancellable, and must never hold focus or keep a second data owner.

A separate, clearly secondary archive retains the former display for compatibility/component testing. It is not loaded by the new default and is not a substitute for new-default QA. Asset failure keeps the new bright paper/identity and original work controls, rather than reverting to dark castle art or inventing zero records.
